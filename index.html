FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is my code stored on a server?+

No. Your code is processed in the browser, and autosaved data stays on this device.

What diagrams can I create?+

You can use Mermaid syntax for sequence, flowchart, class, state, ER, Gantt, pie, and more.

Can I edit downloaded SVG files?+

Yes. Open SVG files in Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape, or another vector editor.
